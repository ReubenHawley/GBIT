**Task** · Feature: #10 (F04) Conformance fixtures and scenarios · Epic: #2 (E1) Graph kernel and conformance suite

## Summary
Prove the safety invariant with a property-based test.

## Architecture acceptance criteria served
- AC-05: Transitive impact
- AC-06: Precise selection
- AC-10: Safe fallback
- AC-13: Reproducibility

## Requirements
- Generate random graphs, change sets and edge additions.
- Property: adding a possible dependency edge, all else equal, yields a superset of the previous selection.
- Use FsCheck or CsCheck with a fixed seed in CI and a random seed nightly.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The property passes for at least 10,000 generated cases.
- [ ] Failing cases are shrunk and printed as fixtures.

## Test-first plan
Write these before the implementation:
- The property test itself, plus a deliberately broken planner to prove it can fail.
